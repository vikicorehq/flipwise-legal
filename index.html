Flipwise

Know what it’s worth before you buy it.

Point your camera at any yard-sale, thrift, or estate-sale find. In seconds, Flipwise tells you what it is, what it typically resells for, and — at that asking price — a straight BUY or PASS with your estimated profit.

The scan: instant ID, typical resale range, honest confidence, and red-flag warnings for fakes.

The verdict (Pro): BUY / MAYBE / PASS, profit math after fees, sold comps one tap away, and ready-to-paste listing copy.

The Flip Log: every find tracked from spotted to sold, with your all-time profit scoreboard.

Estimates are AI-generated guidance, not guarantees — every valuation shows its confidence and links to real sold comps. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
